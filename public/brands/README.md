# Brand assets

Logos used to identify the selected company in this independent educational simulator. The brands and their logos belong to their respective owners.

- FedEx: [SVG source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:FedEx_Corporation_-_2016_Logo.svg), [original SVG](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b9/FedEx_Corporation_-_2016_Logo.svg).
- TXU Energy: [SVG source on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:TXU_Energy_Logo.svg), [original SVG](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/1/1e/TXU_Energy_Logo.svg).

Original SVG colors and proportions are preserved. A white background keeps both logos readable in the dark interface. Assets are bundled locally and do not make runtime requests to the source websites.
